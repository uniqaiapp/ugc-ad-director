Voiceover and captions

Voiceover script

  • 0s If onions make you cry, watch this.
  • 2s The knife way: minutes of stinging eyes and pieces the wrong size.
  • 7s Pull the cord. Pull. Pull. Pull. Lid stays on, so the onion gas stays in.
  • 15s Lid off, into the pan. Even dice, dry eyes.
  • 20s Three parts, all top-rack safe. The blade cartridge comes out as one piece, so nothing to pick at.
  • 26s Link's below. It takes one onion to convince you.
