Pawcam: what the dog does at 2pm

What the dog does at 2pm.

The generated camera feed has no timestamp overlay, so it cannot pass for real footage. The real device appears in shot 5. Separation anxiety and vet wording stay out.

Voiceover and captions

Voiceover script

  • 0s What the dog does at 2pm.
  • 2s You leave at nine and spend the day wondering if he's fine, bored, or eating the sofa.
  • 8s Two taps and you're looking at him. Say his name and the ears go up.
  • 15s Hit the treat button. He forgives you for leaving.
  • 21s Treat hopper, microphone, speaker. Plugs in, joins your wifi, no hub.
  • 26s See what yours does at two. Link below.

66 words over 30s (2.2 words/s).

Compliance notes

  • AI label on screen: AI-generated ad
  • Platform AI-content toggle: on
  • Presenter: ai generated; fictional, not modelled on any real person
  • The pet-cam feed shots are generated and labelled; the real device appears in shot 5 so the hardware shown is the hardware shipped.
  • No separation-anxiety or veterinary wording; the ad sells reassurance for the owner, not a treatment for the dog.
  • Generated feed has no timestamp overlay so it cannot be mistaken for a real recording of a real home.
