Bitewell chews: the breath test (real owner, signed release)

POV: your dog's breath enters first.

This is the one script where a real owner speaks in the first person, because there is a signed release and proof of purchase behind it. The paid partnership is disclosed in speech and on screen. Only shot 4 is generated and its label says b-roll, and there is no vet recommendation and no plaque percentage.

Voiceover and captions

Voiceover script

  • 0s POV: your dog's breath enters first.
  • 2s I've tried brushing. She will not let me, so her breath cleared the sofa every night.
  • 8s One chew a day. She thinks it's a treat. The ridges scrub the back teeth while she gnaws.
  • 15s Chicken-free, sized for medium dogs.
  • 19s Three weeks in, I can sit next to her again. Ingredients are on the back, nothing I can't pronounce.
  • 26s Paid partnership, but the sofa thing is true. Link below.

Compliance notes

  • AI label on screen: AI-generated ad (b-roll)
  • Platform AI-content toggle: on
  • Presenter: real person; real person with a signed release
  • Real customer: signed release and proof of purchase on file; paid partnership stated in speech and on screen (FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR Part 255, 2023 revision).
  • Only shot 4 is generated; the label says b-roll so viewers do not read the presenter as synthetic.
  • No 'vet recommended' or plaque-reduction percentage: no study on file, so the script stays with what the owner saw.
