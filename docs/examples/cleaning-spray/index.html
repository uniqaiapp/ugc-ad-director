Clearstone: the hob in fifteen seconds

Burnt-on hob. Watch the wipe.

At 15 seconds this is the shortest script and it has no voiceover, so the captions carry the whole ad. The generated demo shows the intended use and the proof is real footage. The surface list names glass and induction only.

How does this home cleaning UGC ad script work?

Clearstone Hob Spray runs 15 seconds and opens with a result first hook, “Burnt-on hob. Watch the wipe.” Satisfying-clean format: the viewer stays for the reveal, no talking needed. Of its 5 shots, 2 are generated and 3 are real footage, screen recording or still. It passes lint, and each generated shot has Kling and Veo 3 prompts.
