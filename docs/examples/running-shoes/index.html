Stride Two: the insole test

Your shoe's too narrow. Here's the test.

The hook hands the viewer a check they can run right now, the insole test: pull the insole out and stand on it. No injury or podiatrist wording appears anywhere. The generated running shots start from product photos used as reference frames.

How does this running shoes UGC ad script work?

Stride Two runs 30 seconds and opens with a test hook, “Your shoe's too narrow. Here's the test.” Offers a quick self-diagnosis; the viewer wants the test more than the shoe. Of its 6 shots, 5 are generated and 1 are real footage, screen recording or still. It passes lint, and each generated shot has Kling and Veo 3 prompts.

Voiceover and captions

Voiceover script

  • 0s Your shoe's too narrow. Here's the test.
  • 2s Toes hanging over the insole means the shoe is too narrow. That hole? Width, not mileage.
  • 8s Same foot on the Stride Two insole: toes inside the line. The knit upper stretches, so nothing rubs.
  • 16s On the road your toes spread when you land. This shoe leaves room for that.
  • 22s The fitting is printed on the box. Check before you run.
  • 26s Do the insole test tonight. Link below.
