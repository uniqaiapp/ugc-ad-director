Roamly eSIM: landed, connected, before the bags

Don't buy the airport SIM.

No price is spoken or captioned, and no real airline, airport or carrier branding shows up in the generated shots. The one claim is that there is no contract. The two screen recordings, the install flow and the plan page, are real, so the app shows up exactly as it is.

How does this travel esim UGC ad script work?

Roamly runs 30 seconds and opens with a pattern interrupt hook, “Don't buy the airport SIM.” Direct instruction against the viewer's default habit, said where the habit happens. Of its 6 shots, 4 are generated and 2 are real footage, screen recording or still. It passes lint, and each generated shot has Kling and Veo 3 prompts.

Voiceover and captions

Voiceover script

  • 0s Don't buy the airport SIM.
  • 2s Queue, passport, the tiny tray tool, and your home SIM ends up in a jacket pocket forever.
  • 8s Before you fly: open the app, tap the country, tap install. The eSIM sits in your settings next to your normal SIM.
  • 16s Then you land, airplane mode off, and the map loads at the gate while everyone else is in the queue.
  • 22s Data, days and price are on the plan page before you pay. No contract.
  • 26s Install it before your next flight. Link below.

Compliance notes
