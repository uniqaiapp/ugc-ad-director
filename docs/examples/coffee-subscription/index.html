Roastday: the date on the bag

Find the date on your coffee bag.

A comparison hook sets up a demo the viewer can see for themselves: the bloom, in place of taste claims. The roast date is on the bag and the ship date is on the box. The script promises no delivery time.

Voiceover and captions

Voiceover script

  • 0s Find the date on your coffee bag.
  • 2s A best-before date says nothing about when it was roasted.
  • 6s Fresh beans bloom when the water hits. Stale ones sit flat. That's the whole difference.
  • 12s Roast date on the bag, ship date on the box. Do the maths.
  • 17s First bag without a subscription lock-in. Link below.
