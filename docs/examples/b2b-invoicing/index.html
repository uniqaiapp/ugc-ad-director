Ledgerly: the invoice that chases itself

You don't need to chase that invoice.

An objection hook opens a script about reminders that go out on days seven, fourteen and twenty-one, and that schedule is listed as a claim with evidence. The reminder flow is a real screen recording, while the paid notification is generated. Nothing in it promises faster payment.

How does this b2b saas UGC ad script work?

Ledgerly runs 30 seconds and opens with a objection hook, “You don't need to chase that invoice.” Removes the dreaded task in the first line; the viewer wants to know how. Of its 6 shots, 4 are generated and 2 are real footage, screen recording or still. It passes lint, and each generated shot has Kling and Veo 3 prompts.

Voiceover and captions

Voiceover script

  • 0s You don't need to chase that invoice.
  • 2s Every late invoice means three awkward emails, and you still sound like you're begging for your own money.
  • 8s Send the invoice, switch reminders on, pick friendly or firm. It emails the client on day seven, fourteen and twenty-one.
  • 16s This is what the client gets. Plain, polite, invoice attached, pay button on top.
  • 21s You find out when it's paid. You never write the email.
  • 26s Free for your first five invoices. Link below.
