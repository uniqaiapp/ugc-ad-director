Glass-skin serum: the two-finger test

Your foundation isn't the problem.

A fictional presenter holds up a flaky makeup sponge and talks to the camera as a demonstrator, never as a customer. The split screen in shot 4 shows how the serum goes on, so it works as an application demo and not as a result claim. The AI label is part of the proof, not something to hide.

How does this skincare UGC ad script work?

Dewline Barrier Serum runs 30 seconds and opens with a pattern interrupt hook, “Your foundation isn't the problem.” Names the symptom the viewer already blames on the wrong thing. Of its 6 shots, 5 are generated and 1 are real footage, screen recording or still. It passes lint, and each generated shot has Kling and Veo 3 prompts.

Voiceover and captions

Voiceover script

  • 0s Your foundation isn't the problem.
  • 2s Dry skin drinks the water out of your makeup, so it cakes by lunch.
  • 7s Two drops. Press it in with two fingers, don't rub. Ceramides fill the cracks, niacinamide calms the red.
  • 15s Same foundation, same sponge. Left side had the serum, right side didn't.
  • 20s Fragrance-free, three ceramides, two percent niacinamide. It's all on the label, no mystery blend.
  • 26s Link's below. Try it under your usual base for a week.

75 words over 30s (2.5 words/s).
