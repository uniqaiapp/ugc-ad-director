UGC ad hook library

Hooks for the first two seconds of a UGC ad, grouped by product category. Each one is 12 words or fewer, written to be said and captioned at once, and works with a demonstrator presenter.

What makes a UGC hook work in 2 seconds?

A UGC hook works in 2 seconds when it carries one idea in 12 words or fewer, said and captioned at once, and the first shot ends by 2 s. Lint fails a longer hook or a later cut. Name the viewer's own situation or show the payoff, and skip generic openers such as "Stop scrolling".

Hooks to avoid

  • "Stop scrolling", "wait", "you need this": generic, platform-flagged as low quality, and they say nothing about the viewer.
  • "I've been using this for…", "honest review", "not sponsored": first-person customer experience; with an AI presenter it is a fabricated testimonial and lint rejects it.
  • Brand name first. Nobody knows it yet.
  • Questions with an obvious yes ("Want clearer skin?"). Replace with a check the viewer can run (TEST) or a specific symptom (CO).
  • Numbers you cannot back ("lose 5 kg", "90% said"). They must sit in claims with evidence or they do not ship.

Use a hook

Put the hook in hook.text and in the first caption, then lint the script. The checker flags a hook over 12 words or a first shot that ends after 2 seconds.
