How does a UGC ad script checker work?

A UGC ad script checker reads a script and runs lint rules on it: a hook of 12 words or fewer that ends by 2 seconds, five beats in order, an on-screen AI label, claims backed by evidence and no fake testimonials. It then writes a first-frame image, Kling and Veo 3 prompt for every generated shot.

How it works

  1. Load or write a scriptPick one of the 11 examples, start from a blank script or edit the JSON. A script has a hook, five beats in order, captions, a voiceover and a source for every shot.
  2. Fix what lint flagsErrors block the script: a missing AI label, a fake testimonial, an unlisted claim, a hook over 12 words. Warnings are worth a check: pace, caption length, the platform switch.
  3. Copy the promptsEach generated shot gets a first-frame image prompt, a Kling prompt and a Veo 3 prompt. Copy one shot or all of them, with the character count beside each.

Questions

How long should a UGC ad hook be?

Keep it to 12 words or fewer and make the first shot end by 2 seconds. The hook is said and captioned at once, because the sound is often off. Lint fails a longer hook, and the hook library has 63 starting points across 10 product categories.

Why does an AI-generated ad need an on-screen label?

Generated footage needs a plain label, such as AI-generated ad, in a caption from the first frame. The label must be in a caption because the app's own buttons can cover a corner. Lint fails an empty label, and it warns when the platform's AI-content setting is not marked as switched on.

What counts as a fake testimonial?

An AI presenter that says it used the product, such as "I've been using this" or "changed my life". Star ratings and "verified buyer" wording are claims about real people too. An AI presenter is a demonstrator: it can show, explain and compare. A real customer can testify with a signed release, proof of purchase and a paid-partnership disclosure.

Kling or Veo 3 for a shot with dialogue?

Veo 3 prompts include a Dialogue line when the shot has a voiceover, the presenter is not none and on_camera_speech is not false. Kling prompts describe the visuals only, so record or synthesise the voiceover separately and lay it over the clip.

Is the checker free, and does it send my script anywhere?

It is free. The code and examples are MIT and the vocabulary in references/ is CC BY 4.0. Page views and button clicks are counted anonymously; your script text never leaves the browser. Generating the video elsewhere may cost credits, depending on the tool.

Does a clean lint mean the ad is compliant?

No. Lint checks the rules in this repo and nothing more. Platform policies and laws change, and the notes were last checked on 2026-10-28. Treat compliance.md as a working checklist, not legal advice, and re-read each platform's current ad policy before you run an ad.
