# Hook library

Hooks for the first two seconds, grouped by product category. Each is ≤ 12 words, written to be said and captioned at once, and works with a demonstrator presenter (no "I've used this"). Swap the bracketed part. Types: PI pattern-interrupt, CO callout, CT contrarian, RF result-first, CU curiosity, CM comparison, OB objection, POV, TEST (a check the viewer can do now).

## Hooks to avoid

- "Stop scrolling", "wait", "you need this": generic, platform-flagged as low quality, and they say nothing about the viewer.
- "I've been using this for…", "honest review", "not sponsored": first-person customer experience; with an AI presenter it is a fabricated testimonial and `lint` rejects it.
- Brand name first. Nobody knows it yet.
- Questions with an obvious yes ("Want clearer skin?"). Replace with a check the viewer can run (TEST) or a specific symptom (CO).
- Numbers you cannot back ("lose 5 kg", "90% said"). They must sit in `claims` with evidence or they do not ship.
