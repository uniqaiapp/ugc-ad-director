# Tongue: ten minutes of Spanish you actually say

**Product:** Tongue — speaking-first Spanish course built around ten-minute daily conversations  
**Vertical:** online course · **Platforms:** tiktok, reels, shorts · **Length:** 45s · **Aspect:** 9:16  
**Audience:** adults 25-45 who have restarted a language app three times  
**Presenter:** ai generated — fictional man, early thirties, hoodie, at a kitchen table with headphones; generated, not based on anyone

## Hook (2s)

> Stop learning Spanish. Start saying it.

Type: contrarian. Flips the thing the viewer thinks they are doing wrong (not enough study) into the real gap (no speaking).

## Voiceover script

- **0s** Stop learning Spanish. Start saying it.
- **2s** You can hold a long streak and still freeze when a waiter asks a question. A streak is not a conversation.
- **10s** Every lesson is a ten-minute conversation. It asks, you answer out loud, it corrects the one word you got wrong and keeps going.
- **20s** Day one is ordering a coffee. You do it badly, it nudges you, you do it again. That's the whole method.
- **28s** Thirty situations you'll actually hit: cafe, pharmacy, taxi, the landlord. Each one is a conversation, not a word list.
- **38s** The first three conversations are free, no card. Link below. Say something today.
